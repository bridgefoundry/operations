BRIDGE FOUNDRY, INC.

BOARD OF DIRECTORS

MEETING MINUTES

October 8, 2017

Board Members Present: Melissa Herico, Karen Herreid, and Sarah Allen

Guest Present: Ilen Zazueta-Hall, Bridge Foundry Program Board Chair

The Aug 07, 2017 minutes were reviewed and accepted unanimously.

This meeting of the Bridge Foundry Board of Directors was held on Sunday, October 8, 2017, with everyone participating by online video-conference with live audio and screen sharing.

The board discussed the following topics:

  • Now that we have received the IRS 501c3 determination letter and D\&O insurance, we will begin transferring Bridge Foundry program operations from School Factory to Bridge Foundry, Inc. CEO will delegate operations to COO to streamline the process, adopting additional operational policies to on-board Bridge Foundry volunteers and proceed with fund-raising efforts.

  • Sarah plans to delegate more to Diana to have more time for fund-raising

  • Risks if we don’t:
    • Interruption of service
    • Loss of funding (end-of-year gift giving, MS fiscal started in July, Sept/Oct prime corp fundraising time)
  • Opportunity:
    • Volunteers do more when they know they have a bank account to put sponsor $ in and backup sponsorship
    • We believe our new initiatives will have high impact and we want to spend Board of Directors / Program board energy on getting those started
    • Volunteer training, etc. (next ElixirBridge Nov 4)
    • Branding
    • Bridge Troll improvements
  • Banking Policy - Reviewed and Adopted General Signatory And Banking Authority Policy and confirm Board responsibilities with regards to disbursement amounts. The Treasurer shall be the one (1) officer who may open accounts.
  • The board unanimously approved the opening of a bank account, according to vendor selection guidelines.
  • Strategic Plan draft review (Ilen)

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 7:50pm.

Respectfully submitted,

_______

Recording Secretary
